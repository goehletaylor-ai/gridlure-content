Gridlure Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

The short version: Gridlure doesn't collect any personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking. Your puzzles, streaks, and stats stay on your device.

What stays on your device

Your progress, solved puzzles, streaks, stats, times, and settings are saved only on your device. They aren't sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting Gridlure deletes them.

Downloading puzzles

To get new daily puzzles and collections, Gridlure downloads puzzle files from a website hosted on GitHub Pages. This works like loading any web page: your device requests the files, and GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) as described in GitHub's Privacy Statement. Gridlure doesn't send anything about you or your play with these requests, and we don't receive or use those logs.

Purchases

Gridlure Plus is purchased through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment, and we never see your payment details or Apple Account information. The app only checks with Apple whether Plus has been purchased, so it can unlock it. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

No tracking, ads, or analytics

Gridlure doesn't include advertising, analytics, or tracking tools, and doesn't track you across other apps or websites.

Crash reports and feedback

If you've chosen to share app analytics with developers in your device settings, or you send feedback while testing through TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and that feedback with us. These help fix problems and are handled under Apple's privacy terms.

Children

Gridlure doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
