Gridlure Support

Need a hand? Email taylor@gridlure.com and a person will reply. Including your device and the Gridlure version (Settings → About) helps.

Common questions

When do new puzzles come out?
Two new dailies, a 10×10 and a 15×15, every morning at 6:00 AM Pacific. Solving a daily on its release day keeps your streak going.
I'm stuck on a clue.
Every Gridlure puzzle can be solved with logic alone, with no guessing. Lit-up clues mark rows and columns where something can be settled right now. Hold a lit clue for a few seconds to see which squares; that counts as a hint. Tips & Tricks in Settings explains the rest.
Does my progress carry over to a new phone or iPad?
Yes. With iCloud Sync on (Settings → iCloud), your dailies, Unlimited history, and collection progress follow your Apple Account. Syncing only ever adds; it never removes a solve.
I bought Gridlure Plus but it isn't unlocked.
Open Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Plus is a one-time purchase tied to your Apple Account, and Family Sharing is supported.
A puzzle looks wrong or something crashed.
Please tell us: Settings → Help → Send Feedback opens an email with the version and device already filled in, or write to feedback@gridlure.com.
